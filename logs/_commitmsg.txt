that contain nested badge spans (their text is "Fine DiningOrder"), and
locator().filter({hasText}).first() resolves to the zero-size desktop nav
hidden by 'hidden xl:flex', so waitFor(visible) timed out. getByRole filters
out non-visible candidates. Result: 20/20 surfaces captured with zero page
errors and zero console errors.
